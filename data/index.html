Move

Jog each axis where you want it, then capture the pose with Use as Start / Use as End below. Both axes travel from start to end together and finish at the same time.

Quick Actions

Steppers stay energized (holding torque) by default. Disable to release them so the rig can be moved by hand; any move re-enables them.

Linear Axis LINEAR

mm the carriage travels per full motor turn. Jog speed sets how fast the Left/Right buttons move. Flip direction if LEFT/RIGHT or homing run the wrong way.

Rotary Axis ROTARY

Steps for one full platform turn. Direct drive on a 1/16 NEMA 17 = 3200; raise it for a geared head.

Other Settings

Smooth ramping eases both axes in and out of motion (jog and auto). Turn it off for snappy, constant-speed moves with no acceleration.

All changes are saved automatically.

Step 1 — Home the slider

The carriage drives to the limit switch to find its 0 mm reference. Keep the rail clear.

Step 2 — Measure the rail

Jog the carriage to the far end of the rail, stop just short of the hard stop, then store the distance as the travel limit.

0.0 mm
stored limit 300 mm · jog 30 mm/s

Step 3 — Home the pan

This axis has no limit switch, so you set its reference by hand. The pan motor is released — turn the phone holder to face forward, then tap Set Home.

Forward becomes 180°, the middle of the pan range — that leaves a half turn of travel to either side of it.
